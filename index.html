Reflection Prompt (Write in your class notes):

Why does Washington Irving place the tale inside four distinct narrative layers rather than simply presenting it as a straightforward ghost story? How does this structure shape the reader's trust in the story?

Key Literary Insights to Copy Into Notes:
  • Folkloric Realism: Multi-layered narration imitates authentic oral tradition and regional folklore passed down across generations.
  • Ambiguity & Unreliability: Filtering the narrative through multiple storytellers creates artistic distance, leaving room for mystery regarding whether the event was supernatural or human trickery.
  • Satirical Frame: Attributing the notes to an eccentric antiquarian allows Irving to satirize human superstition while shielding himself from criticism.
